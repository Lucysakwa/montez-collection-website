Our Story

Meet Lucy Jahenda

Fashion and Beauty was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves to feel extraordinary. Founded by Lucy Jahenda, our brand is a curated destination for premium fashion, luxury accessories, and beauty essentials.

Based in Kahawa Wendani, Nairobi, we source the finest pieces — from authentic sneakers and luxury timepieces to elegant jewelry and designer accessories. Every item is handpicked to ensure it meets our standard of excellence.

At Fashion and Beauty, we don't just sell products — we help you tell your story through style. Whether you're stepping out for a casual day or making a grand entrance, we have the perfect piece for you.
